A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Good questions, honest answers.

Everything you need to feel right at home in the Vocalis studio.

What is Vocalis?＋

Vocalis is a creative studio for designing expressive AI voices and turning your words into speech. Describe the voice you imagine, generate it, and explore it in the playground.

Can I try Vocalis before creating an account?＋

Yes. You can create one voice as a guest in the studio. Register or sign in when you’re ready to keep your voice in your personal library and download it.

How do credits work?＋

Credits are used for voice generation and speech synthesis. Buy a one-time credit pack in KES using M-Pesa or a payment card. Your balance and activity are available on your dashboard.

How can I pay?＋

Pay with an M-Pesa STK push, Paybill, Till or QR code, or use card checkout. Credits are added after the payment provider confirms your payment.

Can I edit my voice later?＋

Yes. Edit the voice description in your library, then regenerate it to explore the updated direction. You can also adjust speech settings for each playground session.

What can I do with an album?＋

Albums help you organize your voices around a project or use case. Create an album and add or remove any of your saved voices.
